Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information the RACEUR™ iOS app ("RACEUR", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. RACEUR is operated from New York, USA. Questions: raceursupport@gmail.com.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details. If you create an account: your email address, password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it), username, and optional bio and profile photo. You can also use the app with an anonymous account.
  • Livestream settings. Stream keys, RTMP URLs, your Twitch username and YouTube video ID are stored only on your phone. We do not upload your stream keys.

2. Information collected when you use the app

  • Location. With your permission, RACEUR uses your phone's GPS (including in the background during an activity) to show speed, distance, pace and your map, and to run overtake and checkpoint races.
  • Camera and microphone. Used to show the HUD over your camera and to record or stream video with audio. Recordings are stored on your phone. They're saved to your Photos library or shared only when you choose to.
  • Activity results. When you finish a session we save a summary to your account: score, overtakes, distance, duration, a general location name (for example "Bronx, NY"), and the date.

3. What other RACEUR users can see

  • Live position in overtake mode. While overtake mode is on, your username, live location, speed, heading and activity type are shared with nearby RACEUR users so you can race each other. This stops and your live entry is deleted when you turn overtake mode off.
  • Cat Raceur lobbies. In a multiplayer race, everyone in the lobby sees your username and checkpoint progress.
  • Leaderboards and Route Boss. Your username and scores appear on leaderboards. If you claim a "Route Boss" spot, that session's route, score, speed, distance and time are visible to other users near that area.
  • Livestreams. Anything you stream is sent to the platform you choose (for example Twitch, YouTube, TikTok or Kick) and is public under that platform's terms.

Tip: the "Hide Start & End" setting trims the beginning and end of saved routes so your home or work location isn't shown.

4. Services we use

We share only the information each service needs to work:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions): stores your account, sessions, leaderboards and multiplayer data.
  • Mapbox and OpenStreetMap tile servers: deliver map images. Like any website, they receive your IP address and the map area being viewed.
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap): receives an approximate area around you to find nearby streets for rivals and checkpoints.
  • Twitch and YouTube: used to show your live chat on the HUD while you stream.
  • Apple: location, camera, Photos and Live Activities run through iOS.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. How long we keep it

Account, session and leaderboard data are kept until you delete them or your account. Live multiplayer positions are removed when you leave overtake mode. Videos stay on your device unless you share them.

6. Your choices

  • Turn off location, camera, microphone or Photos access anytime in iOS Settings → RACEUR. Some features won't work without them.
  • Edit your username, bio and photo in your profile.
  • To delete your account and its data, email raceursupport@gmail.com from your account email. We'll complete it within 30 days.

7. Safety

RACEUR is a training and entertainment tool. Obey traffic laws and event rules, mount your phone securely, and don't interact with it while moving.

8. Children

RACEUR is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, protected by access rules that limit it to signed-in users. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
