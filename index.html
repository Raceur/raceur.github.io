THE PLATFORM

One app. Your training, your races, your content.

01

TRAIN

Every run, ride and drive gets an opponent. Rivals appear on your route and push your pace in real time.

  • AI rivals in Pacer, Racer and Elite tiers
  • Overtake combos that reward sustained effort
  • Daily challenges, streaks and personal bests
02

RACE

Bring the crew. Start a race with a six-letter code and see who knows the streets.

  • Cat Raceur: alleycat-style checkpoint races
  • Live riders nearby to overtake head to head
  • Leaderboards and Route Boss spots to defend
03

CREATE

Your phone becomes the camera, the dashboard and the broadcast truck.

  • Racing-style HUD over live camera
  • HUD and raw footage recorded at the same time
  • Go live to Twitch, YouTube, TikTok or Kick with chat on screen

MODES

Built for how you move.

RUN

Chest-mount friendly HUD for road races, 5Ks and city long runs.

RIDE

Handlebar-ready for group rides, crits and alleycats.

CIRCUIT

Landscape dash view for track days, karting and moto.
